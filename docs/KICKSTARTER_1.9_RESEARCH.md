# Assessment design and research

The candidate’s 2027 invitation supplies the scope and clocks for this edition. Optiver describes its Amsterdam Career Kickstarter as a five-day programme for final-year students. The public role description provides programme context. It does not provide the item bank, score cutoffs or a NumberLogic topic distribution. [Optiver programme](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/jobs/trading/amsterdam/career-kickstarter-trading-2027/)

| Assessment | What the invitation establishes | How the Kickstarter trainer implements it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beat the Odds | Apply probability theory under an overall 25-minute clock and a per-question limit. | 20 multiple-choice questions, a 90-second item timer and forward navigation. New questions vary the information revealed, the sampling procedure, the payoff or the stopping condition. Some use one result as the input to a second question. |
| NumberLogic | Numerical reasoning for 25 minutes. | 26 questions with free navigation. Sequence patterns remain prominent. Regular timed and all-skills mixed practice now include forward and reverse percentages, rates, units, weighted tables, unknown outputs, yield and mixture constraints. |
| Likelihood List | Rank events from most to least likely using supplied information. Around 25 minutes overall with an item limit. | 15 rankings, a 90-second item timer, forward navigation and exact-order scoring. Compare alternative reports, population weighting, close rates, histogram area and combinations of experiments with different denominators. |
| Intervals | Select lower and upper bounds to express numerical estimates and uncertainty in about 15 minutes. | 18 items under one 15-minute clock. Timed questions now include published reference quantities, construction of estimates from assumptions and sample-based estimates of simulated populations. The trainer awards lower divided by upper for a covered positive interval. |
| Orderbooks | Find profitable buying and selling combinations in about 15 minutes. | 20 boards. Regular papers now include consistent markets, prices that favour selling a bundle and prices that favour buying one. Use executable bid/ask quotes, cancel all exposures and observe the six-unit limit on new boards. A pass is a final timed decision. An unsuccessful position can be revised after a trainer time penalty. |
| Zap-N | One or more neuroassessment games lasting 2–15 minutes each. | Nine existing trainer games. Three introductory games cover comparison, working memory and switching rules. The remaining six follow as familiarisation choices. Game-specific scores are reported separately. |

The detailed counts, 90-second item clocks, navigation, negative marking, trade penalties, target scores, reserve-paper clocks and training mixture are OptPrep settings. The invitation confirms an item limit in two sections without giving its length. Its approximate overall estimate should be used for scheduling, alongside the individual section durations and the breaks it permits. The portal’s instructions govern the real assessments.

## Why these design choices

A commercial practice provider describes NumberLogic as sequence recognition and Beat the Odds as timed multiple-choice probability. That supports retaining a strong sequence component and practising probability under a clock. The invitation describes numerical reasoning more broadly, so the app lets the learner choose the balance. The default 35% applied share is an editorial training choice. The provider also describes event ranking, interval estimates, executable combinations and several distinct cognitive-game skills. [Tradermath assessment overview](https://www.tradermath.org/practice/firms/optiver)

Jane Street’s guide is an independent source of practice in probability, expected value and markets. Those principles informed the curriculum. Its interview problems have their own purpose, pacing and difficulty. No equivalence between a level in OptPrep, a Jane Street problem and an Optiver cutoff has been established. The trainer uses original scenarios and separately authored reserved questions. [Jane Street Probability & Markets](https://www.janestreet.com/probability-markets/)

## Coach and progress

Today gives different suggestions when the evidence supports them. Four or more recent focused and mixed observations of one skill can reveal a gap between executing a known method and selecting it under mixed conditions. Repeated correct answers over pace support speed work. Low unaided focused accuracy supports relearning. Calculation and input slips can be tagged in the timed debrief. A missed multiple-choice answer on its own is ambiguous. Where evidence is thin, the plan identifies the skill for practice without attributing a cause.

The planner also uses recent timed runs on separate London practice days, workshop completion and first-attempt paper results. All three fixed papers stay outside the normal generators. Historical scores remain available. Older interval results lacking width measurements keep their original scores and do not contribute invented values to the width chart.
